Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Shotbee ("the app") is made by Hasan Yılmaz, an individual developer in Warsaw, Poland ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: your health data stays on your phone. There is no account, no ads, and we do not sell or share your data.

1. Data you enter stays on your device

Everything you log in Shotbee (medication and dose, shots and injection sites, weight, side effects and check-ins, notes, protein and water, goals, reminder times) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

It leaves your phone only when you choose: for example when you export a CSV file, create a backup file, share a progress card or share a doctor report. Where that file goes (email, cloud drive, messages) is up to you and is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted from your phone. Phone-level backups (iCloud or Google device backup) may include it, under Apple's or Google's terms.

2. Purchases (Shotbee Pro)

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. To know whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). It receives an anonymous app user ID created by the app, your purchase and subscription history from the store, your device type, system and app version, country and IP address. RevenueCat does not receive your health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

3. App updates

The app checks for small updates through Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA). The check sends technical information only, such as app version and platform, plus your IP address as part of any internet connection. See Expo's privacy policy.

4. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent through our servers.

5. Emails to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and the app version and phone system the app adds to the email. We use this only to answer you and keep it no longer than needed (at most 2 years).

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No use of your data to train AI.

7. Legal basis and your rights

We process the limited data in sections 2, 3 and 5 to provide the app and your purchases (contract) and to answer you (legitimate interest). You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of data we hold about you, or object to processing, by emailing getshotbee@gmail.com. We never hold the health data you log, so you control it fully in the app (edit or delete entries, or delete the app). You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Poland that is UODO (uodo.gov.pl). Some providers above are in the USA; transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

8. Children

Shotbee is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
