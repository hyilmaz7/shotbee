Shotbee support

Shotbee is a simple tracker for weekly GLP-1 shots: shots and injection sites, reminders, weight, side effects, protein and water. Your data stays on your phone.

Need help?
Email getshotbee@gmail.com. We usually answer within 2 working days. In the app you can also use Me › Help › Email support, which adds your app version so we can help faster.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy. Use Me › Back up to a file to keep a copy, and Restore from a backup on a new phone.

How do I cancel Shotbee Pro?
iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Shotbee. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Tracking stays free forever.

I bought Pro on another phone.
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple or Google account.

Reminders don't arrive.
Check that notifications are allowed for Shotbee in your phone's settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not hiding them.

Is Shotbee medical advice?
No. Shotbee is a log and reminder tool. It does not calculate or recommend doses. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
